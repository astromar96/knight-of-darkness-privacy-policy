Knight of Darkness — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Knight of Darkness is a gothic pixel-art action-platformer for Android and iPhone, published by Pixelz Apps. This policy explains exactly what the game does with data on your device.

Knight of Darkness collects no personal data, and nothing you do in the game leaves your device. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and the developer operates no server. On Android it does not even request permission to use the internet.

What stays on your device

The game keeps two small files in its own private storage. Neither is transmitted anywhere, and the developer has no way to see them:

DataWhy the game keeps it
Your save Your progress: the levels you have cleared and your results in them, the embers you have banked, the rites you have learned and the upgrades you have sworn at the Oath.
Your settings Music and sound volume, the touch-control layout, button size and opacity, haptics and screen shake.

Permissions

On Android the game asks for one permission, vibration (VIBRATE), for haptic feedback in combat and menus. You can turn haptics off under Options. It requests nothing else: no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

What is never sent

Nothing. The game sends no data about you, your device or how you play to the developer or to anyone else.

The app stores

Downloading the game from Google Play or the App Store is handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. The developer may see the aggregate, anonymous statistics those stores provide to every developer, such as the number of installs by country and crash rates reported by the device's operating system.

Children

Knight of Darkness is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Data retention and deletion

Your save and settings stay in Android's or iOS's private storage for the game until you remove them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes both. Starting a new game replaces your save.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be noted in the release notes on the store.
